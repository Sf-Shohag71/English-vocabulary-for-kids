1. How can I start learning English on this website?
You can start by exploring our beginner lessons, interactive exercises, and quizzes. We also offer structured courses to guide you step by step.
2. Is this website free to use?
Yes, all lessons and learning materials are completely free to use.
3. Do I need to create an account?
No, you can start learning right away without signing up or logging in
4. How can I build my English vocabulary?
Choose a lesson level and learn new words with their meanings, pronunciation, and example sentences. Practice a few words every day, review them regularly, and try using them in your own sentences when speaking or writing. Small, consistent practice works better than studying a lot in one sitting.
5. Do you offer certificates for completed courses?
Currently, we don’t offer certificates. Our focus is on helping you learn and practice English at your own pace.
